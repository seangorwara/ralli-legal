Ralli Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Ralli Sports (“Ralli,” “we”) makes Ralli, a social app where sports fans post bet slips, follow each other, and build a track record called the Ralli Rating. Ralli is not a sportsbook. It never takes bets, handles money, or connects to your sportsbook account. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Who can use Ralli

You must be 21 or older to use Ralli.

What you give us

  • Account info: your email address, password (stored encrypted; we can’t see it), and username. You can also tell us who invited you.
  • Profile info: an optional photo, bio, and favorite teams.
  • Bets you post: the team, odds, game, and any amount you choose to show. We use these to grade results and calculate your Ralli Rating. The Rating never uses bet amounts.
  • Bet slip screenshots: when you post from a screenshot, the image is stored privately. Only you can see it, and we can see it to check posts. Other users only see the bet details.
  • Social activity: comments, likes, follows, and what you view in Pulse. People whose moments you view can see that you viewed them.
  • Feedback: messages and screenshots you send us through the app.
  • Onboarding answers: for example, your guess of your own Rating.

What we collect automatically

  • Usage events: for example, which onboarding screens were viewed. They’re linked to your account and a random ID stored on your phone. We never include bet amounts.
  • Notification info: if you allow notifications, your phone’s push address and time zone, so we can send them at reasonable hours.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, or advertising ID, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Services we use

These companies process data only to run Ralli:

  • Supabase: our database, login, and file storage.
  • Anthropic: reads your bet slip screenshot so the bet can be filled in automatically. Anthropic’s terms don’t allow it to use this data to train its models.
  • Amplitude: app usage analytics.
  • Expo and Apple: deliver push notifications.

We also use public sports data, like game times and scores, to grade bets. That doesn’t involve your personal data.

What others can see

Your username, photo, bio, favorite teams, posted bets, Ralli Rating, record, badges, comments, likes, and follows are visible to other Ralli users. Your email address and slip screenshots are never shown to other users.

What we never do

  • We don’t sell your personal data.
  • We don’t use it for ads.
  • We don’t send you messages that encourage betting.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. That permanently removes your account, profile, posts, comments, photos, and slip screenshots. To also delete your analytics data, email us.

Keeping data safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by account, so private items like slip screenshots can only be opened by their owner.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
